# AyuSakshi Authoritative Knowledge Base Registry
SIH26045 - Official Source Inventory

This knowledge base repository contains authoritative legal statutes, rules, treaties, pharmacopoeial standards, and regulatory guidelines for Ayurveda IP & Compliance across Indian and International regimes.

## Directory Structure
- `india/patents/`: Patents Act 1970 (Section 3(p), Section 2(1)(j), Section 10), Patents Rules 2003/2024.
- `india/trademarks/`: Trade Marks Act 1999 (Classes 5, 3, 30, 44), Trade Marks Rules 2017.
- `india/gi/`: Geographical Indications of Goods Act 1999, GI Rules 2002.
- `india/designs/`: Designs Act 2000, Designs Rules 2001 (Ayurvedic packaging, applicators, delivery devices).
- `india/copyright/`: Copyright Act 1957, Copyright Rules 2013 (Ayurvedic databases, compilations, software).
- `india/biodiversity/`: Biological Diversity Act 2002 (2023 Amendment), Biological Diversity Rules 2004 & 2024 (ABS obligations, Form A/B/I/II, AYUSH exemptions).
- `india/plant-variety/`: PPVFR Act 2001, PPVFR Rules 2003 (Medicinal plant varieties, farmers' rights).
- `india/ayush/`: Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 (Chapter IV-A, Ayurvedic/Siddha/Unani drugs, Sections 33A to 33N), Drugs and Cosmetics Rules 1945 (Rules 151-170, First Schedule Texts), Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API), Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI), Drugs and Magic Remedies Act 1954.
- `india/fssai/`: Food Safety and Standards Act 2006, FSSAI (Ayurveda Aahara) Regulations 2022.
- `international/`: TRIPS Agreement, CBD, Nagoya Protocol, WIPO GRATK Treaty (2024), PCT, Madrid Protocol, Hague Agreement, Budapest Treaty.
- `case-law/selected-cases/`: Landmark judgements on Traditional Knowledge, Section 3(p), Biological Diversity Act enforcement, and Ayurvedic trademark protection.
