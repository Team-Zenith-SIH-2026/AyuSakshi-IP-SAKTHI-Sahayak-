# Authoritative Knowledge Base Inventory

The knowledge base implements the curated official source list for SIH26045 across Indian and International regimes:

## 1. Indian IP & Regulatory Corpus
- **Patents Act, 1970** & **Patents Rules, 2003 / 2024**: Section 3(p) (traditional knowledge non-patentability), Section 3(e) (mere admixture & synergistic efficacy), Section 3(d), Section 10(4) (biological source origin disclosure).
- **Trade Marks Act, 1999** & **Rules, 2017**: Nice Classification Classes 5, 3, 30, and prohibition against monopolizing generic Sanskrit Ayurvedic medicine names.
- **Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999** & **GI Rules, 2002**: Regional terroir, authorized user rights (e.g. Kashmiri Saffron, Malabar Pepper).
- **Designs Act, 2000** & **Rules, 2001**: Ayurvedic delivery applicators, novel dispenser packaging.
- **Copyright Act, 1957** & **Rules, 2013**: Ayurvedic databases, software compilations, clinical manuscripts.
- **Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (Amended 2023)** & **Rules 2004 / 2024**: Section 6(1) NBA IP clearance, Section 7 SBB intimation, Section 3 foreign access, Form A/Form I/Form III, registered Vaidya exemptions.
- **PPVFR Act, 2001** & **Rules, 2003**: Plant variety protection for medicinal botanicals and farmers' seed rights.
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940** (Chapter IV-A) & **Rules, 1945** (Rules 151-170): Classical formulations (First Schedule texts: Charaka, Sushruta, AFI, API), Patent & Proprietary (P or P) medicines, Phytopharmaceuticals (Rule 122E, Schedule Y).
- **Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954**: Advertising restrictions on therapeutic disease cures.
- **Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahara) Regulations, 2022**: Dietary and nutritional food products prepared under classical Ayurvedic processes with mandatory logo and disclaimer.

## 2. International Treaties & IP Corpus
- **WIPO GRATK Treaty (2024)**: Mandatory patent disclosure of genetic resources and traditional knowledge.
- **Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)** & **Nagoya Protocol**: Prior Informed Consent (PIC) and Mutually Agreed Terms (MAT) for access and benefit sharing.
- **WTO TRIPS Agreement**: Article 27.1 & Article 27.3(b) (patent exclusions and sui generis plant protection).
- **Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT)**: Unified global patent filing across 157+ countries.
- **Madrid System & Hague Agreement**: International brand trademark & design filing systems.
- **Budapest Treaty**: International deposit of microorganisms for patent procedures.
